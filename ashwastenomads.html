Ash Waste Nomads

Gangs of the Outlands, Ash Waste Nomad gang list and equipment reference. Stat lines are copied from the book. Rule text is summarised in my own words. Not in the scanned pages: the Unbound Helamite and Ashwing Helamite profiles, and the Ash Waste Nomad Equipment List prices. Those rows are marked, and need adding from the book. The skill-access table was read from a skewed photo, so check it.

Gang special rules

  • Ghosts of the Wastes: at the start of the battle, before the first Priority roll, two Ash Waste Nomad Fighters may swap positions on the battlefield. Nomad Fighters ignore both difficult and dangerous terrain.
  • Natives of the Wastes: the gang may not include any Vehicles or Mounts that are not in its Equipment List.
  • Starting skills: Leaders and Champions may select one skill from one of their Primary skill sets.

Fighter rules

  • Chieftain: may purchase weapons and wargear from the Ash Waste Nomad Equipment List, and during a campaign from the Trading Post.
  • Watcher: may purchase weapons and wargear from the Ash Waste Nomad Equipment List, and during a campaign from the Trading Post.
  • Stormcaller: equipped with a Dustback Helamite (included in its cost). While equipped with a Stormcaller staff, an Active Stormcaller can perform the Dust Storm action. Dust Storm (Single): if the Visibility (X″) rule is not active, apply the Visibility (24″) rule. Otherwise, increase or decrease X″ by 6″, to a minimum of 6″. The effect lasts for the rest of the battle and can be changed by future uses of this ability.
  • Warrior: may purchase weapons and wargear from the Ash Waste Nomad Equipment List, and during a campaign from the Trading Post.
  • Hunter: starting skill is Infiltrate.
  • Dust Runner: may purchase weapons and wargear from the Ash Waste Nomad Equipment List, and during a campaign from the Trading Post.
  • Arthromite Duneskuttler: Hard to Kill. When rolling on the Lasting Injury table, roll twice and apply whichever result the owning player wishes. Skills: Clamber. Armed with mandibles; may not purchase or be equipped with additional weapons or wargear.
  • Arthromite Spinewyrm: Leash range 6″. Small Target: ranged attacks against it are made at -1 to Hit. Burrowing: it may move freely under impassable terrain if it has enough movement, but may not end its movement on or within impassable terrain. Armed with barbed fangs; may not purchase or be equipped with additional weapons or wargear.

Mounts

Dustback Helamite (exclusive): a Fighter equipped with a Dustback Helamite gains the Mounted and Agile subtypes and the Mighty Leap skill, and its Movement becomes 8″. When it performs a Dash action, it adds 4″ to its Movement instead of its Initiative. The Dustback Helamite is equipped with Helamite claws, which gain the Additional Attacks (1) trait.
