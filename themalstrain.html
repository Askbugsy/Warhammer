The Malstrain

Gangs of the Outlands, Malstrain gang list and equipment reference. Stat lines are copied from the book. Rule text is summarised in my own words.

Gang special rules

  • Madness of the Malstrain: the gang may only recruit models listed in this gang list, unless the book states they will join Malstrain gangs.
  • Starting skills: Leaders and Champions may select one skill from one of their Primary skill sets.

Fighter rules

  • Malstrain Alpha: may perform the Fade action (Double) during its activation, removing itself from the battlefield. At the start of the next round it returns using the Infiltrate skill. It may not perform Post-cycle Actions. Skills: Clamber, Dodge, Infiltrate, Juggernaut, Nerves of Steel and Rain of Blows. Armed with razor-sharp claws and a venomous bite.
  • Malstrain Genestealer: may not perform Post-cycle Actions. Skills: Clamber, Dodge, Juggernaut, Nerves of Steel and Rain of Blows. Armed with razor-sharp claws and a venomous bite.
  • Malstrain Coalescence: treats Malstrain Wyrd Powers as a Primary skill set. It may not perform Post-cycle Actions. Armed with feeder tendrils.
  • Malstrain Brood Scum: may purchase weapons and wargear from the Malstrain Equipment List and, during a campaign, from the Trading Post.
  • Malstrain Tyramite: Leash range 6″. Skills: Dodge. Armed with a stinger.
  • Pets (Coalescence, Genestealer and Alpha) may be given additional Pets purchased from the Malstrain Equipment List during a campaign. A Coalescence or Tyramite cannot be equipped with additional weapons or wargear.

Malstrain Wyrd Powers

Malstrain Coalescences and any Malstrain Corrupted gang Leader upgraded to a Wyrd have access to these powers, which count as a Primary skill set.

  • 1. Catalyst (Free), Continuous: A friendly Fighter within 12″ of the Wyrd that is taken Out of Action may perform a Shoot or Fight action as a Free action before being removed.
  • 2. Hypnotic Gaze (Double): An enemy Fighter within 9″ and in line of sight must make an Agility test. On a fail it loses its Ready marker.
  • 3. The Horror (Single): The targeted enemy Fighter within 12″ must immediately make a Nerve check.
  • 4. Leech Essence (Single): Make an attack at an enemy Fighter within 12″ and in line of sight: Str 3, AP -1, L1 Hit. If it takes any damage, the Wyrd may regain a Wound lost earlier in the battle.
  • 5. Paroxysm (Single), Continuous: While maintained, the Wyrd sets its BS and WS to 6+ against an enemy Fighter within 9″ and in line of sight.
  • 6. Aura of Despair (Free), Continuous: While maintained, enemy models within 12″ of the Wyrd reduce their Cool by 1.

Malstrain Corrupted gangs

Will of the Patriarch: during gang creation a player may decide their gang has been corrupted by the Malstrain. The six Clan Houses (Cawdor, Delaque, Escher, Goliath, Orlock and Van Saar) and the Palanite Enforcers can be corrupted.

  • Effects: members lose the gang's special rules except Starting Skills and Skill Access. The Leader may be upgraded to a Wyrd, and Malstrain Tyramites may be purchased by Leaders and Champions.
  • Upgrading the Leader: a Wyrd upgrade costs +35 credits and can be paid during the Post-cycle Sequence, but the Leader forgoes Post-cycle Actions and must not be in Recovery. Once upgraded, the Leader gains the Wyrd subtype and one Wyrd Power, and counts Malstrain Wyrd Powers as a Primary skill set.
  • Recruiting: up to three Malstrain Genestealers and one Malstrain Coalescence may be added to the gang. Leaders and Champions may each take up to two Malstrain Tyramites. Any number of Malstrain Brood Scum may be added.
  • Restriction: no Fighters with the Brute, Hanger-on or Pet subtype may be purchased from the gang's list.
  • Genestealer Cult corrupted gangs: cutting ties with the original gang means they may not add Brute or Hanger-on fighters, nor purchase Pets from their Equipment List.
