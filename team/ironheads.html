Ironhead Squat Prospectors

Gangs of the Outlands, Ironhead Squat prospectors list. Stat lines are copied from the book. Rule text is summarised in my own words. Weapon profiles are in the section at the bottom.

Gang special rules

  • Dauntless: Ironhead Squats don't need to take Nerve checks when a friendly fighter goes Out of Action within 3″.
  • Starting skills: when added to a roster, Leaders and Champions can pick one skill from one of their Primary skill sets.

Fighter rules

Charter Master, Drill Master, Exo Master

Equipment is taken from the Ironhead Squat list. The Charter Master can't pick Exo weapons. The Drill Master can't pick Exo weapons either. The Exo Master comes with an exo-suit included in the cost, and can only pick from the Exo Weapons and Power Pack sections, plus the Trading Post.

Drill-Kyn, Digger

The Drill-Kyn can't pick Exo weapons. The Digger can't pick Exo weapons.

Exo-Kyn

Starts with the Nerves of Steel skill and comes with an exo-suit included in the cost. Weapons come only from the Power Pack and Exo Weapons sections, plus the Trading Post.

Vartijan Exo-Driller

Sensor Suite: it treats the Visibility (X″) rule as 3″ higher than it is. Starts with the Bulging Biceps skill. Armed with a power fist, heavy flamer and seismic crusher. It can swap the heavy flamer for a heavy bolter (+10 Cr). It can't buy other weapons or wargear.

Techmite Exovator

Leash range 3″. Can carry a maximum of two weapons (bi-arm). Weapons come from the Power Pack section and the Trading Post.

Techmite Oculi

Leash range 12″. Target Paint: while at least one Oculi is within 6″ of an enemy fighter, its owner counts as firing at short range for cover bonuses. Armed with pincers. It can't buy other weapons or wargear.

Skalvian Explorator

A tracked vehicle armed with a Skalvian flamer, a Skalvian heavy stubber, an Ironhead claw and a Skalvian hull. It can't buy additional weapons or wargear.
