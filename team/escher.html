House Escher

Gangs of the Underhive, House Escher gang list and equipment reference. Stat lines are copied from the book. Rule text is summarised in my own words.

Gang special rules

  • Nimble: Escher fighters may re-roll a natural 1 on any Agility test.
  • Chem-Alchemy: Escher gangs can craft custom Elixirs, Toxic Ammo (for Toxin weapons) and Gaseous Ammo (for Gas weapons) during the post-battle sequence, paying the credit cost listed in the book for each dose or batch.
  • Starting skills: when added to a gang roster, an Escher Leader or Champion can pick one skill from one of their Primary skill sets.

Fighter rules

Escher Gang Queen

Equipment is taken from the House Escher list. Extra weapons and wargear can be given during a campaign, from the list or the Trading Post.

Escher Gang Matriarch

Equipment is taken from the House Escher list, with extra weapons and wargear from the list or the Trading Post during a campaign.

Escher Death-Maiden

Poison Blood: a Death-Maiden's weapons with the Toxin (X+) trait are stronger. When a Death-Maiden rolls a natural 1 to Wound with one of them, it can re-roll the die.

Escher Gang Sister, Wyld Runner, Little Sister

The Wyld Runner starts with the Clamber skill. Equipment is taken from the House Escher list, with extra kit from the list or the Trading Post during a campaign.

Khimerix

Regeneration: an active Khimerix can take the Regenerate action. It moves up to its Movement characteristic, then rolls a D6. On a natural 4 or more it recovers one Wound lost earlier in the battle.

Armed with chemical cloud breath and talons. It can swap the breath for a gaseous eruption breath (+25 Cr) or the talons for razor-sharp talons (+25 Cr). It can't take other weapons or wargear.

Phyrr Cat

Leash range 9″. If it falls for any reason, the distance it falls counts as 3″ shorter on the Falling table. Armed with talons. It can't take other weapons or wargear.

Phelynx

Leash range 3″. Armed with claws. It can't take other weapons or wargear.

Mounts

Escher Cutter: a fighter with one gains the Mounted and Flying subtypes and a Movement of 9″. On a Dash action it adds 5″ to its Movement, in place of its Initiative. The Cutter carries Cutter grenade launchers with frag and krak grenades. It can swap them for Cutter heavy stubbers (+10 Cr) or Cutter plasma guns (+15 Cr), when added to the roster or in any post-cycle sequence.
