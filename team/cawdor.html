House Cawdor

Gangs of the Underhive, House Cawdor gang list and equipment reference. Stat lines are copied from the book. Rule text is summarised in my own words. The skill-access table is left out, as noted at the end of the page.

Gang special rules

  • A House Divided: when a Cawdor gang is founded, it follows either the Path of the Pious or the Path of the Fanatic.
  • Path of the Pious: the leader must have the Pious subtype. If the leader dies, a Fanatic fighter can't replace them. A Fanatic can't be added if it would take the Fanatic count above the Pious count. Without Number: at the end of the Choose Crews step, the starting crew can include one extra Prospect.
  • Path of the Fanatic: the leader must have the Fanatic subtype. If the leader dies, a Pious fighter can't replace them. A Pious can't be added if it would take the Pious count above the Fanatic count. Fanatical: re-roll Bottle checks of a natural 11 or 12.
  • Starting skills: when added to a roster, Leaders and Champions can pick one skill from one of their Primary skill sets.

Fighter rules

Cawdor Way-Brethren

Starts with the Dodge skill. Comes with a Ridge Walker included in the starting cost. Weapons and wargear come from the House Cawdor list.

Cawdor Stig-Shambler

Starts with the Bulging Biceps skill. Armed with a heavy club and a heavy flamer. It can swap the heavy flamer for a twin-linked heavy stubber (+10 Cr). It can't buy other weapons or wargear.

Sheen Bird

Leash range 6″. Subject to the Frenzy condition. Armed with beak and talons. It can't buy other weapons or wargear.

Redemptionist fighters

Priest, Deacon, Brethren and Zealot take their equipment from the Redemptionist list, with the same campaign extras.
