Venators

Gangs of the Underhive, Venator gang list. Stat lines are copied from the book. Rule text is summarised in my own words. Several fighters pick one profile from a set, so each set is listed in full.

Gang special rules

  • Claiming Bounties: if a Venator gang has taken at least three enemy fighters Out of Action by the Receive Rewards step of the Post-battle Sequence, it adds 20 credits to its stash.
  • Starting Skills: Venator Leaders and Champions may select one skill from one of their Primary skill sets.
  • Skill Access: when creating a Venator gang, pick any four skill sets from the Necromunda Skirmish: Core Rulebook page 150 and rank them 1 to 4 (1 embodies the band as a team best). Access is then set by the table below, noted separately.

Subtypes

Ogryn

Starting Skills: in addition to any other skills, all Ogryns have the Juggernaut skill.

Ratling

  • Sniping: may re-roll Hit rolls of a natural 1 when performing an Aimed Shot action.
  • Sneaking: always counts as being in a weapon's Long Range when calculating the bonus for cover.

Beastman

Feral Warriors: Beastman fighters increase their Attacks characteristic by 1 while they are Injured.

Gang legacy

Most fighters in a Venator gang have access to a Gang Legacy, as detailed in their fighter datasheet. A fighter with a Gang Legacy may purchase weapons and wargear from its gang's equipment list when added to a gang. It may not purchase additional weapons from that list during the Post-cycle Sequence. A Venator Hunt Leader or Hunt Champion may also purchase Pets associated with its Gang Legacy when added to a gang roster (for example, a Goliath legacy may take a Sumpkroc). Gangs may mix Gang Legacies freely, but each fighter may only originate from one gang.

Equipment limits

  • Hunt Leader: may purchase from its Gang Legacy list and the Trading Post with a combined TP of 5 or less.
  • Hunt Champion: TP of 4 or less.
  • Hunter: TP of 3 or less.
  • During a campaign, each may be given extra weapons and wargear from the Trading Post. It may not buy additional weapons or wargear from its Gang Legacy list.
