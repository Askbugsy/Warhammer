Outcasts

Gangs of the Outlands, Outcast gang list. Stat lines are copied from the book. Rule text is summarised in my own words.

How an Outcast gang is built

An Outcast gang has no fixed fighter list. The Leader can be any profile you choose, and each gang picks an Affiliation (where the Leader came from) and an Archetype (how they lead). Both are fixed once chosen.

  • Gang special rules: Cult of Personality: Outcast fighters within 6″ of the Leader automatically pass Nerve checks and ignore the Fearsome condition. Lead the Masses: each Outcast Champion needs at least three Hive Scum in the gang. If not, Champions are removed from the roster until it's fixed.
  • Starting skills: when added to a roster, Leaders and Champions can pick one skill from one of their Primary skill sets.
  • Designer's note: any fighter from another gang list can be promoted to Leader, keeping its skills and equipment, and taking an Affiliation and Archetype. The arbitrator makes the final call.

Affiliations

  • Clanless: each Leader and Champion gets an extra 1 TP to spend on Trading Post weapons and wargear at creation. The first Visit Trading Post action in each post-cycle sequence earns an extra 1 TP.
  • Clan House: choose Cawdor, Delaque, Escher, Goliath, Van Saar or Orlock. Leaders and Champions can buy weapons and wargear normally restricted to that house's list, including pets, at creation and in each post-cycle sequence.
  • Aranthian: Leaders and Champions can buy from the Aranthian equipment list at creation and in each post-cycle sequence.
  • Mutant: fighters can buy mutations from the list below. Leaders and Champions can have up to two, and gangers only one.
