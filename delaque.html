House Delaque

Gangs of the Underhive, House Delaque gang list and equipment reference. Stat lines are copied from the book. Rule text is summarised in my own words.

Gang special rules

  • Psychoteric Choir: while a Delaque fighter is within 3″ of two or more other friendly Delaque fighters, its Leadership and Willpower are each improved by 1.
  • Starting skills: when added to a roster, Delaque Leaders and Champions can pick one skill from one of their Primary skill sets.

Fighter rules

Master of Shadow and Phantom

Master of Whispers: when added to a roster, the fighter may gain the Wyrd subtype and choose a Psychoteric Whispers Wyrd Power for +35 Cr.

Nacht-Ghul

From the Shadows: instead of deploying normally, it can be placed to one side. From the start of a round after the first, its controlling player can set it up anywhere that isn't in line of sight of an enemy, and not within 9″ of any enemy fighter.

Ghost, Psy-Gheist and Spektors

The Ghost and Psy-Gheist use Whispers in the Dark: the Psy-Gheist selects a Psychoteric Whispers Wyrd Power when added. The Piscean Spektor does the same, and is armed with psychomantic claws only. It can't buy other weapons or wargear.

Cephalopod Spektor

Leash range 3″. Sensor Array: if it is within leash range of its owner when the owner must make an Intelligence check for any reason, roll an extra D6 and discard one of the dice. Armed with shock tendrils. It can't buy other weapons or wargear.

Psychoteric Wyrm

Leash range 12″. Psychoteric Node: when a Delaque Wyrd uses a Psychoteric Power, it can channel that power through an Active Psychoteric Wyrd within leash range, measuring range and line of sight from the Wyrm. Burrowing: it can move freely under impassable terrain, but can't end its move in impassable terrain. Armed with ferocious jaws. It can't buy other weapons or wargear.

Psychoteric Whispers (Wyrd Powers)

A Wyrd treats these as a Primary skill set. Summaries are in my own words.

  • Terrible Truths (Single): an enemy fighter within 6″ and in line of sight makes a Willpower check. On a failure it gains the Insanity condition.
  • Psychotic Lure (Single): an enemy fighter with a Ready marker within 6″ and in line of sight is activated next by the opposing player. It can't perform Group Activation when next activated.
  • Deceitful Thoughts (Double): an enemy fighter within 6″ and in line of sight makes a Willpower check. On a failure, the Wyrd's controlling player can move it up to its Movement in any direction, without moving it into harm.
  • Cacophony of Silence (Single, Continuous): while maintained, enemy fighters within 6″ and in line of sight must re-roll successful Hit rolls for ranged attacks.
  • A Perfect Void (Free, Continuous): while maintained, the Wyrd counts as always in cover, and as within a weapon's Long range to all enemy fighters within 10″.
  • Eternal Slumber (Single): a Seriously Injured enemy fighter within 3″ and in line of sight of the Wyrd immediately goes Out of Action.
