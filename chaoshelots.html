Chaos Helots

Gangs of the Outlands, Chaos Helot cult list and equipment reference. Stat lines are copied from the book. Rule text is summarised in my own words.

Gang special rules

  • Blessed by the Chaos Gods: Chaos Helot cults can perform Chaos Rituals (see the Chaos Rituals section below).
  • Starting skills: Leaders and Champions may select one skill from one of their Primary skill sets.

Chaos Rituals

A Chaos Helot cult can perform two Post-cycle Actions. The Lead Ritual (Leader only) selects one of the Chaos gods (the Blood God, the Plague Lord, the Dark Prince or the Architect of Fate) and rolls 2D6 with modifiers for the gang's campaign results, reputation and existing favour. A result of 9 or more earns the god's favour, which is marked on the gang roster and gives a bonus in the gang's next battle. A natural double 1, or a final result of 2 or less, makes the cult unworthy: it loses any favour it had, and any fighter that performed Ritual Focus turns into a Chaos Spawn. The Ritual Focus action (one fighter maximum) adds further effects and can give D3+3 XP to another fighter that performed it.

Favour bonuses: Blood God re-rolls one failed Wound roll per round; Plague Lord re-rolls one Recovery roll per round; Dark Prince lets two Ready models activate one after the other during the Activation phase, neither performing the usual Group Activation action; Architect of Fate re-rolls one Hit roll per round.

Chaos Helot Wyrd Powers

Cult Witches have access to these powers, which count as a Primary skill set.

  • 1. Scouring (Free), Continuous: While maintained, the witch counts as armed with the Scouring weapon above.
  • 2. Levitation (Free), Continuous: While maintained, the witch's Movement increases by 3″ and it gains the Flying subtype.
  • 3. Warp Strength (Free), Continuous: While maintained, the witch's Strength increases by 2 and the Lethality of its close combat attacks increases by 1.
  • 4. Warp Shield (Single): Until the end of the round, the witch and friendly fighters within 3″ add 1 to their Save.
  • 5. Maddening Visions (Single): Until the end of the round, enemies within 3″ of the witch make a Willpower check when they end an activation there. On a fail they gain the Insanity condition.
  • 6. Assail (Single): Make a ranged attack at an enemy within 12″ and in line of sight. On a hit, move the target D3″ and Suppress it. Contact with another fighter causes Str 3, AP -, L1 hits; if the two fighters belong to different gangs, both become Engaged instead of Suppressed.

Chaos Corrupted gangs

The six Clan Houses and the Palanite Enforcers can be Chaos Corrupted. Their members lose most special rules but keep Starting Skills and Skill Access. The gang picks one Chaos god at creation, may perform both Lead Ritual and Ritual Focus, and may add any number of Cult Witches and up to one Chaos Spawn. Leaders and Champions may take a Chaos Familiar each. Brute, Hanger-on and Pet fighters from the gang's own list are not available.
