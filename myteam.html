BC

The Blades of the Crown

Forged in the neon-lit chem-spires of Hive Primus, the Blades of the Crown are a regally ruthless House Escher court draped in royal plum silk, hammered gold filigree, and synthetic venoms distilled in their own hidden alchemical sanctuaries. Where rival gangs rely on brute bulk or rusted iron, the Blades rule the upper gantries through precision toxin, vertical mastery, and calculated assassination.

“Every crown is bought in gold; every throne is kept in venom.”
👑 Crown
HM
Her Majesty
The Sovereign of Spires • Gang Queen (245 Cr)
Boltgun • Silent Needle Pistol • Mesh & Undersuit
A cold-eyed strategist who treats every sump skirmish like a royal execution. Bearing an Old Battle Wound from the spire wars that placed the golden diadem on her brow, she commands from behind cover—orchestrating lethal slingshot strikes with a flick of her gloved hand before finishing survivors with whisper-quiet toxin darts.
“Do not waste breath threatening them, darling. Simply point out where they are about to fall.”
Signature Aesthetic: High royal plum collar, gold crown circlet, tailored flak-silk coat, pearl-inlaid needle pistol.
CH
Copperhead
Mistress of the High Perch • Gang Matriarch (335 Cr)
Suspensor Heavy Stubber • Lasgun • Spring Up
An iron-nerved veteran gunner whose burnished copper-and-gold hair stands like a beacon atop the highest gantry towers. Thanks to her gyro-stabilised Suspensor rig and feline Spring Up reflexes, not even heavy suppressing fire can keep her down for long.
“Take the high gantry, sisters. Everything below the railings is just targets.”
Signature Aesthetic: Burnished copper mane, gold-trimmed heavy stubber harness, elevated sniper stance.
MW
Miss Whips
The Gantry Warden • Gang Sister (75 Cr)
3" Versatile Shock Whip • Critical Injury Survivor
Scarred by a near-fatal Critical Injury early in the campaign, Miss Whips turned survival into sadistic artistry. She patrols narrow catwalk chokepoints beside Her Majesty, cracking her arc-charged Shock Whip across three paces to stun charging brutes dead in their tracks.
“One step closer to the Queen and I’ll peel the armour right off your ribs.”
Signature Aesthetic: Coiled gold-handled electro-whip, plum-and-brass bracers, fierce scarred smirk.
SC
Scorpio
Right Flank Sentinel • Gang Sister (55 Cr)
Lasgun (+1 Short Acc, Plentiful)
Sharp-tongued and fiercely loyal, Scorpio guards the secondary ladder approaches to Copperhead’s sniper roost, punishing any flanker foolish enough to climb into her crosshairs.
“Climb if you like—it’s a long drop back to the sump.”
Signature Aesthetic: High braided crest, gold shoulder pauldrons, braced lasgun posture.
TL
Talon
Gantry Acrobat • Little Sister Juve (45 Cr)
Toxin Stiletto Knife • Stub Gun • Fast Learner
Fearless across narrow beams and broken walkways, Talon uses her 6" sprint and 3+ Initiative to out-manoeuvre sluggish Goliath and Squat gunlines and secure distant loot caskets.
“Too slow, heavy-boots!”
Signature Aesthetic: Plum scarf trailing in the spire draft, gold-hilted stiletto, stub gun drawn.
🐍 Venom
BW
Black Widow
The Silk Executioner • Death-Maiden (295 Cr)
3" Versatile Stiletto Sword • Chem-Synth • Displacer Field
Brought back from the brink of chem-death and bound to Her Majesty’s court, Black Widow glides across catwalk railings like a phantom. Her Combat Virtuoso bladework lets her strike rivals across three paces of open air—delivering hyper-concentrated Chem-synth toxins before enemy blades can even rise to parry.
“Three paces is more than enough room for a corpse to kneel.”
Signature Aesthetic: Obsidian-and-plum respirator veil, glowing green chem-vials, shimmering Displacer field aura.
NS
Nightshade
First Marksman • Gang Sister Specialist (55 Cr)
Precision Lasgun • Specialist
Elevated to Specialist for her unerring accuracy in dim sump-light, Nightshade anchors the court’s mid-tier firing line—pinning enemy heavy gunners in place so Black Widow can advance unseen.
“Keep their heads down; Widow is already behind them.”
Signature Aesthetic: Plum-tinted targeting visor, gold-etched lasgun barrel, poised marksman stance.
MB
Mamba
Left Flank Sentinel • Gang Sister (55 Cr)
Lasgun (+1 Short Acc, Plentiful)
Relentless and disciplined, Mamba holds the left gantry flank alongside Nightshade, laying down rhythmic las-volleys to strip enemy Overwatch before close-quarters assaults begin.
“Hold the lane. Nothing crosses this bridge standing.”
Signature Aesthetic: Emerald-and-gold serpent motifs woven into royal plum combat leathers.
VX
Vixen
Spire Runner • Little Sister Juve (45 Cr)
Toxin Stiletto Knife • Stub Gun • Fast Learner
The boldest of the court’s three initiates, Vixen sprints up unguarded ladders to claim upper-tier objectives and finish pinned heavy-armoured rivals with her venom-coated stiletto.
“Big armour just means a bigger prize for the Queen!”
Signature Aesthetic: Agile runner boots, plum sash, twin hip holsters and glowing stiletto blade.
