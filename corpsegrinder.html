Corpse Grinder Cults

Gangs of the Underhive, Corpse Grinder Cult gang list and equipment reference. Stat lines are copied from the book. Rule text is summarised in my own words.

Gang special rules

  • Rage-filled Minds: Corpse Grinder Cult fighters gain an extra 1 XP for taking an enemy fighter Out of Action during an activation in which they performed a Charge action.
  • Starting skills: Cult Leaders and Champions may select one skill from one of their Primary skill sets.

Fighter rules

Butcher, Cutter and Skinner

These fighters may only be equipped with weapons from the Close Combat Weapons section of the Corpse Grinder Cult equipment list and the Trading Post.

Initiate

Unlike most Prospects, an Initiate is not automatically promoted to Ganger when it reaches 13 XP after the Post-cycle Sequence. The owning player may choose to keep it as a Prospect and receive the usual benefits. If it is promoted, any ranged weapons it carries go into the gang's Stash, and it may then only be equipped with weapons that have the Melee trait.

  • Erratic Movement: roll the Movement characteristic each time the Chaos Spawn is activated.
  • Blessed by the Lord of Skin and Sinew: when taken Out of Action, do not roll on the Lasting Injury table. It is always considered Out Cold.
  • Out of Control: in the Wrap-up step of the Post-battle Sequence, the controlling player nominates up to three fighters that did not go Out of Action. Roll a D6 for each. If any roll is a 4+, the Chaos Spawn is restrained and stays with the gang; otherwise it escapes into the underhive and goes into Recovery while the gang retrieves it. If any fighter rolls a 1, the Chaos Spawn lashes out at it: roll on the Lasting Injury table for that fighter and apply the result immediately.
  • Armed with warped appendages. It may not purchase or be equipped with additional weapons or wargear.

Corpse grinder cult icon

Exclusive: only one fighter in a gang may carry the icon. It must be a Leader or Champion, and no fighter may carry more than one. While the carrier is Active, it may perform the Enrage (Single) action: all friendly fighters completely within 6″ of it that have a Ready marker and are Active add D3″ to their Movement characteristic until the End phase of that round.
