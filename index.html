⚔️ Vertical Underhive Tactical Playbook

Core Battle Plan & 3-Way Crossfire Protocol

  • Core Strategy: Anchor high ground early with Copperhead, control mid-tier approaches with paired Lasguns and Miss Whips, and use Her Majesty's Overseer + Group Activation (2) to launch Black Widow or Toxin juves onto exposed enemies and upper objectives.
  • Avoid the Central Killing Floor Early: Let Blue Harvest's heavy guns/Vandoth and Ryan's Enforcer Bolters/Concussion templates wear down each other's heavy armour first.
  • Anti-Blast Spacing (>3"): Keep fighters spaced more than 3" apart against Blast templates and Enforcer Photon Flash Flares.

Phase 1 — Deployment & Turn 1 (Claim High Ground & Stage)

  • Establish Copperhead's Overlook: Move Copperhead onto a Tier 2 or Tier 3 perch with cover and fall protection. Suspensors + Spring Up let her climb, stand, and unleash Rapid Fire (2) Heavy Stubber bursts freely.
  • Hide Key Strike Pieces: Keep Black Widow and Her Majesty tucked behind solid Tier 1/2 bulkheads out of heavy sightlines while staging within 12" of each other.
  • Split Climb Across Two Routes: Advance Nightshade, Mamba & Scorpio on mid-levels for Lasgun pinning, while Vixen, Fang & Talon sprint up a secondary ladder to avoid bottlenecks.

Phase 2 — Turns 2–3 (Pin Overwatch & Execute the Slingshot)

  • Pin to Block Overwatch (Not Melee Stance): Charging a Prone & Pinned enemy makes them stand up into engagement. Always shoot dangerous shooters with Lasguns before moving to strip Overwatch while Black Widow crosses open lanes.
  • 3-Step Overseer Slingshot: 1) Activate Her Majesty using Group Activation (2) with up to two nearby eligible fighters.
    2) Spend Her Majesty's Overseer (Double) action to grant Black Widow (or an objective runner) two immediate actions up a vertical route into cover.
    3) Immediately take Black Widow's own normal activation in the group sequence to strike before the opponent can respond.
  • Zone Narrow Gantries: Position Miss Whips on catwalk bridges and ladder landings — her 3" Versatile Shock whip hits rushers before base contact.

Phase 3 — Endgame & Capture Window (3" Toxin Strike)

  • Zero Reaction Attacks (3" Combat Virtuoso): Combat Virtuoso gives Black Widow's Stiletto sword Versatile (3"). Move within 3" (with LoS across railings/gaps) and Fight without entering base contact — enemies without a 3" weapon get zero reaction attacks.
  • Subdue Priority Targets Alive: Trigger Chem-synth before striking leaders/specialists. Toxin bypasses Toughness and armour saves completely, sending targets straight to Seriously Injured for clean scenario captures.
  • Lock Down Objectives: Park Fast Learner Little Sisters (Vixen, Fang, Talon) on the upper objective while Copperhead and Lasgunners suppress late counter-pushes.
#1 — Dutch (Champion | Autocannon Knockback, Unwieldy, Grapnel, Cameleoline) Posture: Duck & Cover → Flank
Assigned Hunters: Copperhead (Pin) + Black Widow (3" Strike) / Little Sisters

Top threat + high Champion XP. Eliminating him removes their only long-range gun and stops Knockback ledge drops. Never cross open catwalks in his sightline Turn 1; pin him with Copperhead or lasguns to block Overwatch, then slingshot Black Widow up a blind route for a 3" Versatile strike.

#2 — Quaid (Champion | Plasma Pistol, Autopistol, Gunfighter, Mesh + Undersuit) Posture: Out-Range / Ambush
Assigned Hunters: Copperhead (20"+) / Her Majesty (Boltgun) / Black Widow

Glass cannon (T3, 2W) inside 12" that pays full Champion XP. Out-range him at 12"–24" with Copperhead or Her Majesty's Boltgun, or pin him with a lasgun before Black Widow engages.

#3 — Kali-dont (Hive Scum | Chainsword, Autopistol, Frenzon) Posture: Zone & Counter
Assigned Hunters: Miss Whips (3" Shock) / Lasgun Sisters

Fast Frenzon melee threat (T3, 1W). Intercept him on narrow bridges with Miss Whips' 3" Versatile/Shock reach before his Chainsword can swing, or pin him at range with lasguns.

#4 — Kimble, Harry Tasker, Jack Slater & Ben Richards (1x Specialist + 3x Scum) Posture: Attack Aggressively
Assigned Hunters: Vixen / Fang / Talon + Lasgun Sisters

Soft T3, 1W gangers armed with Autogun & Lasguns. Dropping 2–3 forces early Bottle checks and feeds Fast Learner XP to your Little Sisters.

#5 — Vandoth the Fallen (Bounty Hunter | T4, 3W, Heavy Carapace, Stimm-slugs, Boltgun) Posture: Avoid Early → 3" Toxin Late
Assigned Hunters: Black Widow (3" Versatile + Chem-synth)

Walking fortress (T4, 2+ initial armour, Crushing Blow, Fearsome). Ignore and avoid his sightlines early while he trades shots with Ryan's Enforcers; in the endgame, hit him from 3" Versatile range with Black Widow's Chem-synth + Toxin Stiletto sword to bypass his T4 and 2+ carapace armour completely.

#1 — Palanite Captain / Sergeants (Bolters & Command) Posture: Pin Overwatch → Toxin Assassinate
Assigned Hunters: Lasgun Sisters (Pin) + Black Widow (3" Chem-synth Strike)

Enforcer Bolters (S4, AP-1, D2) shred T3 Escher in open corridors. Keep Her Majesty and Miss Whips out of Bolter lanes, strip Overwatch with Lasgun pinning, and use Black Widow's 3" Toxin strike to bypass their 4+ Flak/Armoured Undersuit saves.

#2 — Subjugators (Vigilance Pattern Shields & Concussion Carbines / Grenade Launchers) Posture: Space >3" & Flank Shields
Assigned Hunters: Black Widow (Flank 3") / Little Sisters (Toxin Knives)

Concussion Blast templates knock fighters off upper gantries and trigger automatic pinning. Keep fighters spaced >3" apart and away from unguarded ledges; flank Vigilance shields with mobile Little Sisters and Toxin knives.

#3 — Palanite Patrolmen (Bolters, Shotguns & Photon Flash Flares) Posture: High-Ground Crossfire
Assigned Hunters: Copperhead (Heavy Stubber) + Nightshade / Mamba / Scorpio

Keep fighters >3" apart so a single Photon Flash Flare cannot blind multiple activists. Use Copperhead's elevated Rapid Fire (2) Heavy Stubber to pin Patrolmen before they climb into short shotgun range.
