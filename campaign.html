🎯 Core Battle Plan & Crossfire Protocol

  • Anchor High Ground Early: Claim Tier 2/3 cover with Copperhead (Heavy Stubber + Suspensors + Spring Up) to suppress heavy guns and shred 1-Wound troops.
  • 3-Way Crossfire Discipline: Never rush the central killing floor Turn 1. Let heavy-armoured rivals burn ammo and wounds on each other while you hold elevated flanks.
  • Template & Flare Spacing: Keep fighters spaced >3" apart (and >5" vs Mark’s Goliath Incendiary Charges) so one Blast or Photon Flash Flare never pins multiple sisters.
  • Pin to Strip Overwatch: Charging a Pinned enemy stands them up into engagement. Always shoot dangerous gunners with +1 Short Acc Lasguns first to cancel Overwatch while Black Widow advances.

🚀 The 3-Step Overseer Slingshot

1. Group Activate: Activate Her Majesty using Group Activation (2) alongside Black Widow (and one bodyguard/runner within 3").

2. Double-Action Launch: Spend Her Majesty’s Overseer (Double) action to grant Black Widow two immediate Move actions up a blind ladder or across a gantry gap.

3. Zero-Reaction Strike: Take Black Widow’s normal activation immediately, trigger Chem-synth, and strike from 3" Versatile range (Combat Virtuoso) with zero enemy reaction attacks.

⏱️ Phase-by-Phase Execution

Phase 1 — Deployment & Turn 1

Perch Copperhead on Tier 2/3 cover away from unrailed edges. Hide Her Majesty and Black Widow behind solid bulkheads within 12" of each other. Split Lasgunners and Little Sisters across two climb routes.

Phase 2 — Turns 2–3 (Pin & Slingshot)

Pin enemy heavy weapons with Lasguns and Heavy Stubber bursts. Zone narrow catwalks with Miss Whips’ 3" Shock Whip, then execute the Overseer Slingshot onto isolated Champions or Leaders.

Phase 3 — Endgame & Capture

Use Black Widow’s 3" Toxin + Chem-synth to send multi-wound elites straight to Seriously Injured for scenario captures, while Fast Learner Little Sisters lock down upper objectives.

🏗️ Terrain Adaptation Matrix

Vertical Sector Mechanicus

Exploit Escher’s 2+/3+ Initiative on climbs and gap jumps (especially vs Mark’s 4" Move Goliaths and Nate’s 5+ Init Squats). Keep fighters 2"+ back from unrailed ledges against Knockback guns.

Zone Mortalis Tunnels

Choke points make Miss Whips’ 3" Versatile Shock Whip and Black Widow’s 3" Stiletto Sword untouchable around corners. Use door terminals to split enemy Group Activations.

Open Ash Wastes / Scatter Ruins

Hug heavy scatter cover Turn 1 so Rav’s Lascannon, Aaron’s Autocannon, and Ryan’s Bolters cannot pick off unarmoured Gangers. Rely on 24" Lasgun pinning until Black Widow closes within 12".
