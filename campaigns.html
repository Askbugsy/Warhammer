🌫️ The Lost Dome (Enter Primus)

A Groxbourne campaign for Necromunda using the N23 rules and the Dominion campaign. Ten games and four one-offs, running October 2026 to March 2027.

Gang: Blades of the Crown (House Escher • Law Abiding) • Owner: Askbugsy

Gang status (v5 printout): Rating 1,440 • Stash 7 Cr • Wealth 1,447 • Reputation 5 • Gang size 10

Territories: Settlement, Settlement, Wastes

Campaign pack: Arbitrator's Advice and Gang Selection are on the pack's sections 3 and 4. Fighters can't be taken above 12 at founding.

⚔️ Occupation: Rounds 1 to 4

Win an Occupation game and you claim a new territory. In Round 1 it's a Drinking Hole in the Fort. In Rounds 2 to 4, roll on the D66 territory table.

Round 1: Bar Brawl (Kick-off day)

Battlefield and crew

  • 3′ × 3′ table laid out as a bar, with tables, a counter and a door or two. Each table is a drinking hole (F1 to F4 on the map).
  • Two to four players per table, drawn at random. Crew is random (7). Skip the dome tables; it's indoors.
  • Deploy by roll-off, within 12″ of the centre and at least 1″ from any fighter already placed.

How it plays

  • Drinking phase: each fighter, Seriously Injured ones included, either takes an Intoxicated token or staggers D6″.
  • Fighting phase: each fighter takes a single Basic, Simple or Double action. A Charge moves D6″ in total.
  • Each Intoxicated token gives +1 Toughness and -1 WS and -1 BS.
  • End phase: Engaged standing fighters are pulled apart by ½″ and take an Initiative test (-1 per token), going Prone and Pinned on a fail. Then Bottle tests and Seriously Injured resolution as normal.
  • Weapons stay at the door. Everyone fights with bar items (Sx3, S-1, D1, Am 5+).
  • Last Orders: after 90 minutes the battle ends and the gang with the most fighters Standing wins. A tie means nobody does.

Rewards

  • Everyone who took part: +1 XP. Each Standing fighter: +1 XP per Intoxicated token.
  • The winner: +D3 Reputation and a Drinking Hole territory.
  • Black and Blue: some Lasting Injuries count as Convalescence instead, and one re-roll of a Lasting Injury is allowed.

Round 2: The Hatch

Battlefield and crew

  • 3′ × 3′ table with a marker or terrain piece in the centre for the hatch. The hatch can be stood on but not passed through.
  • Crew is custom (12). Fighters in Recovery from Round 1 sit this one out.
  • Winner picks a table edge and the loser takes the opposite one. Deploy within 6″ of your edge.
  • Upper Dome tables from the pack's section 8.

Objective

  • From the End phase of Round 3, a gang with fighters within 8″ of the hatch, and the other gang with none, wins. Seriously Injured fighters don't count. A gang also wins if it's the only one with fighters left.
  • Dome Breathes: after two hours the hatch vents and the gang with the most fighters within 8″ of it wins. A tie is a draw.

Rewards

  • Everyone who took part: +1 XP. The winner: +D3 Reputation and a new territory. A gang that bottles out: -1 Reputation.

Round 3: Below the Hatch

Battlefield and crew

  • A Zone Mortalis board if you have one, otherwise a 3′ × 3′ table packed with line-of-sight blockers and no long firing lanes. Warhammer World.
  • Crew is custom (8). Upper Dome tables from section 8.
  • Place five Cache tokens: one in the centre, then the others at least 8″ from any edge and 6″ from each other.
  • Winner picks a corner, loser takes the opposite one. Deploy within 8″ of your corner.

Searching and banking

  • A Standing fighter in base contact with a cache can make a Search (Basic) action: D6 of 1 is a booby trap (Strength 4, AP -1, Damage 1 hit, cache removed), 2 to 5 is salvage, and 6 is a Coalition crate, worth double.
  • A fighter carries one cache. One that goes Seriously Injured, Out of Action or Broken drops it where they stand.
  • A cache is banked when its carrier is in their own deployment zone at the end of an End phase.
  • The battle ends when all caches are banked or removed, or when a gang bottles out or runs out of fighters. Most banked caches wins (a Coalition crate counts as two). Lights Out after two hours: caches being carried count as banked.

Rewards

  • Everyone who took part: +1 XP. Each fighter who banks a cache: +1 XP more.
  • Each banked cache: D6×10 credits. Each Coalition crate: 2D6×10 credits.
  • The winner: +D3 Reputation and a new territory. A gang that bottles out: -1 Reputation.

Round 4: Staking Claims

Battlefield and crew

  • 3′ × 3′ table, Upper Dome tables from section 8. Warhammer World.
  • Crew is custom (10). Everyone else waits in Reserve.
  • Two Beacon objectives, each in the centre of a table edge and 6″ in, facing each other across the table.
  • Mark a 6″ × 6″ zone in each corner. Winner of the roll-off picks a diagonal pair of corners, and the loser takes the other pair. Winner deploys first.
  • Reserves: from the End phase of Round 2, at the end of each End phase each player can bring on D3 fighters from Reserve, touching a table edge inside one of their zones.

Objective

  • A gang controls a Beacon with the most fighters within 1″ of it at the end of an End phase. Seriously Injured fighters don't count.
  • Controlling both Beacons at the end of an End phase scores 1 VP. First to 2 VP wins. A gang also wins if it's the only one with fighters left.
  • Shift's End: after two hours the most VP wins, and a tie is a draw.

Rewards and Downtime

  • Everyone who took part: +1 XP. The winner: D6×10 credits, +D3 Reputation and a new territory.
  • A gang that bottles out: -1 Reputation.
  • Downtime: after your post-battle sequence, every gang gets 250 credits to spend before Round 5. This is the only Downtime in the campaign.
  • The Helm: beat the gang holding the Helm and it's yours. Bring it to Round 5.

🏛️ Takeover: Rounds 5 to 10

From Round 5 territories can change hands. Win and you take one territory of your choice from the gang you beat. Settlements can't be taken. If the beaten gang holds nothing else, roll a new territory instead. Losing costs you one territory and nothing else. Draws change nothing.

Scenario sheets for Rounds 5 to 10 go out one round ahead.

🧭 Campaign rules (Dominion)

Pairings and odd numbers

  • Everyone plays the same scenario each round. Pairings are drawn at random by someone other than the arbitrator, avoiding rematches where possible.
  • With an odd number of gangs, one game each round is a three-player game, drawn at random. Nobody plays a second three-player game until everyone has played one.

Tactics Cards

  • Build a deck of 10 before the kick-off. You can use one per campaign game, and each card works once per campaign.
  • Underdog bonus: for every full 100 Gang Rating you're behind, choose one extra card for that game only.
  • Banned: Dangerous Footing, History of Violence, Effigy of the Damned, every Underdog card, and any card that brings in a Dramatis Personae.

Post-battle sequence

  • Post-battle actions can happen in any order or together.
  • A fighter in Recovery misses the gang's next campaign game. Captured fighters follow the rulebook; a rescue is an extra game and doesn't count as your round game.
  • No House Agents, House Favours, Alliances or sub-plots.

Trading Post and gang rules

  • Only the Trading Post in the N23 rulebook is used. From your first post-battle sequence you can buy from it.
  • Gangs start with 1,000 credits and a Settlement, which can never be taken. Maximum 12 fighters at founding.
  • Basic, Special, Heavy, Unwieldy and Paired weapons must be modelled on the fighter (WYSIWYG).
  • No more than one fighter with the same skill at founding. Brutes can't become Outcast Leaders.
  • Hired Guns: no more than one Bounty Hunter at a time. Hive Scum have no limit.

Table rules

  • Pre-measuring is allowed. Once a model has moved, it can't be moved back.
  • Fighters can leave the table by moving off their own starting edge. They count as Out of Action for Bottle tests.
  • All rolls are made in front of your opponent.
  • Rapid Fire hits can be spread across several fighters.
  • On a 2′ × 2′ table, cut every deployment and objective distance by a third (rounding up) and play any Custom crew two fighters smaller.

Arguments

  • Settle rules disputes with your opponent first. If you're stuck, roll off and keep playing. Questions that matter beyond the game go to the arbitrator afterwards.

🗺️ Territories, Pillar Track and the Helm

Territories

  • Roll a D66 on the territory table when you win an Occupation game in Rounds 2 to 4, and in Takeover when the beaten gang holds only a Settlement. Every territory is available to every gang, and house territories give an extra boon to the matching house.
  • New territories go in a free Shelf or Deeps sector next to one you already hold (the Rim counts). The Deeps open from Round 3. When a territory changes hands, it stays where it is and changes owner.
  • Territory summaries and their boons are in the pack, section 7. Five have been amended for this campaign, and those amendments replace the rulebook.

Dome Environment

  • Before deployment, one player rolls on the Visibility table and the other on the Hazard table. Rounds 1 to 4 use the Upper Dome tables and Rounds 5 to 10 use the Deep Dome tables (pack, section 8).
  • Scenarios that set their own visibility skip the roll, and the one-offs skip both.

Pillar Track

  • Four pillars hold the dome up, with twelve boxes on the map. From Round 5, some scenarios produce a Pillar mark. At the end of each round from 5 to 9, if at least half the games produced a mark, one box is ticked. The finale ticks boxes directly.

The Helm

  • After the kick-off day, the gang holding the most territories takes the Helm. Ties go to Reputation, then a roll-off.
  • From then on, beat the holder in a campaign game and the Helm is yours. It does nothing; everyone can see it.
  • Whoever holds it after Round 10 keeps it until the next campaign.

Awards (after the Climb, 20 March 2027)

  • The Butcher's Bill: most enemy fighters taken Out of Action.
  • Fat Purse: most credits in the stash after Round 10.
  • Landlord: most territories after Round 10.
  • Top Dog: most campaign games won.
  • Big Name: highest Gang Rating after Round 10.
  • Best Painted: voted by the group.
