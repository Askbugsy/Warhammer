Genestealer Cults

Gangs of the Outlands, Genestealer Cult gang list and equipment reference. Stat lines are copied from the book. Rule text is summarised in my own words.

Gang special rules

  • Cult Uprising: fighters add +1 to the dice roll when attempting the Coup de Grâce action.
  • Extra Arm: an option some fighters have, stated on their entry. Those fighters treat the Braced Shot action as a Single action instead of a Double. They can carry four weapons instead of three and always count as armed with a clawed arm (which does not use one of their allowed weapons).
  • Starting skills: Leaders and Champions may select one skill from one of their Primary skill sets.

Fighter rules

  • Adept (Wyrd subtype): treats the Genestealer Cult Wyrd Powers as a Primary skill set.
  • Alpha: an Early Generation Hybrid, so it benefits from the Extra Arm rule.
  • Hybrid Acolyte, Neophyte Hybrid, Hybrid Initiate (Mixed Generations): by default they belong to a later generation. For +20 Cr when added to a roster they can be designated as early generation, which gives them the Extra Arm rule.
  • Aberrant: has the Unstoppable skill. It may not be equipped with ranged weapons.
  • Abominant: has the Unstoppable skill. Armed with a power sledgehammer. It may not purchase or be equipped with additional weapons or wargear.
  • Psychic Familiar: Leash range 3″. Omen of Fortune: while within its owner's leash range, the owner has a 4+ invulnerable save. Precognition: the familiar has a 4+ invulnerable save. Has the Catfall and Clamber skills. It has no weapons and may not purchase or be equipped with additional weapons or wargear.

Cult icon

Exclusive: a gang may purchase only one cult icon. It must be carried by a Leader or Champion. When a fighter carrying it performs the Group Activation action, it may re-roll the Leadership check.

Genestealer Cult Wyrd Powers

Adepts and any Corrupted gang Leader upgraded to the Wyrd subtype can use these powers, which count as a Primary skill set.

  • 1. Hypnosis (Single): Pick an enemy fighter within 9″ that has a Ready marker. It must end its next activation closer to the Adept. If it can't, it takes a Str -, AP -, L1 Hit that ignores saves.
  • 2. Unbreakable Will (Free), Continuous: Friendly fighters within 9″ use the Adept's Cool or Willpower instead of their own for checks.
  • 3. Zealot (Single), Continuous: Friendly fighters within 9″ gain the Hatred (Everything) condition.
  • 4. Mind Control (Single): Pick an enemy fighter within 9″ and in line of sight. It immediately makes a Shoot action at an eligible fighter from its own gang, chosen by the owner.
  • 5. Assail (Single): Make a ranged attack at an enemy within 12″. On a hit, move the target D3″ and Suppress it, with extra Str 3 hits for each fighter it bumps into.
  • 6. Force Wave (Single): Enemy fighters within 3″ are pushed D3+1″ directly away, with a Str 3 hit for any they crash into.

Corrupted gangs

Six Clan Houses and the Palanite Enforcers can be corrupted by a Genestealer Cult. Their members lose most special rules but keep Starting Skills and Skill Access. The Leader may be upgraded to a Wyrd for +35 Cr. Corrupted gangs may add up to two Aberrants and two Abominants, and may buy one Psychic Familiar for each Leader or Champion. Brute, Hanger-on and Pet fighters from their own gang list are not available.
